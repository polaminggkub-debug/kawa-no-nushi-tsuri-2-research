THE CUSTOM FLY MAKER

Body, wing, tail… and a real price quote

These are direct captures of the original Japanese game. In the first-stage shop, we followed the full Mayfly sequence and checked one order against the money counter.

Observed Mayfly palette: 20 wing choices; 9 tail sprites plus a separate “None”. One first-body + first-wing + first-tail order cost ¥25 (¥5 + ¥5 + ¥15). That is one measured combination, not a universal price.

The catalogue contains all 315 named/listed entries: 21 rods, 81 lures, 64 fly-body records, 47 wing records, 23 tail records, 23 baits, 13 hooks, 10 floats/sinkers, 10 foods, and 23 tools/quest items. The 64 bodies + 47 wings + 23 tails account for all 134 fly-table records.

All 134 ROM fly-table records are represented here as 64 bodies, 47 wings, and 23 tails. ID 87 is a no-item code and is excluded.

ROM price fields are not proof of shop stock. A zero field does not establish a free item. Fly-table price values are treated as component fields, not finished-fly retail prices.

The custom fly maker was directly observed in the first-stage shop. Captured sequence: choose family, body, wing, tail or none, then confirm. The observed Mayfly palette had 20 wing choices and 9 tail sprites plus a separate None option. One measured order cost 25 yen (5 + 5 + 15); this quote is not a universal price for all combinations.

The screenshots came from the user-supplied unpatched Japanese ROM in an isolated Snes9x runtime. For hidden items, valid inventory IDs were temporarily written into WRAM so the game itself rendered the selected item. No AI-generated art was used.
