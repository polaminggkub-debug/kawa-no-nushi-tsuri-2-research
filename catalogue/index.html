Choices for this category · 4 sections

Float fishing: which rod should I buy first?

In area 1, choose Clear stream carbon rod 5.3 m for ¥500: it gives more time to aim and a line that breaks less easily than Yamabe rod 3.9 m at ¥350. Yamame rod 4.5 m costs ¥750 but does not give more time to aim than Clear stream carbon rod 5.3 m. In area 3, if you want the most time to aim among float rods and are buying new, Ayu carbon rod 10 m costs ¥1,500, the same full price as Ayu rod 7 m, and is better on both counts.

More time to aim gives you longer to move the target before the game decides where the cast lands. A line that breaks less easily lets the fish pull farther before tackle is lost (this covers only one way of losing tackle).

Area 1 sells rods Yamabe rod 3.9 m, Clear stream carbon rod 5.3 m and Yamame rod 4.5 m; area 3 sells Ayu rod 7 m and Ayu carbon rod 10 m. This does not rank bite or catch odds, and fish can escape other ways.

Lure fishing: where is the useful upgrade?

In area 1, when buying new, choose the large lure rod at ¥600 instead of the small lure rod at ¥500 if you can afford the extra ¥100. If you already own the small lure rod and its aim time is enough, keep using it. In area 4, the heavy-fish lure rod costs ¥650 and has the most time to aim and the line that breaks least easily among lure rods. Buy it for those two things, not for easier bites.

The large lure rod gives more time to aim than the small lure rod; the heavy-fish lure rod adds both more time to aim and a line that breaks less easily.

Casting: start cheap, then upgrade if aim time matters

The small casting rod is the ¥250 starting choice in area 1. If you want more time to aim, skip to the medium casting rod for ¥600 in area 2. The two-handed casting rod at ¥1,500 in area 6 has the most time to aim in this style, but you do not need it if the medium casting rod is enough.

The medium casting rod gives more time to aim than the small casting rod; the two-handed casting rod gives the most among casting rods.

Fly fishing: should I buy the large rod directly?

If you plan to fly-fish and are buying a new rod, buy the large fly rod for ¥450 in area 1. The medium fly rod costs ¥400, but the large fly rod has the most time to aim and the line that breaks least easily. This is not a ¥50 trade-in or upgrade price if you already own the medium fly rod.

The large rod (13) costs ¥450 and the medium rod (12) ¥400; they differ in time to aim and line strength.
