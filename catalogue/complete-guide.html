Complete fishing guide

Five methods, six stages. For each stage: what the shops sell, what to buy or skip, the one easiest kit, and which method suits which fish. Every line shows how far it can be trusted.

How fishing difficulty works Confirmed from the ROM

  1. Every fight starts at a difficulty from 0 (easiest) to 6 (hardest). One step easier gives you one more mistake before the fish gets away.
  2. The rod sets the first step. A light rod makes small fish easier. A heavy rod never makes the start easier. A rod named for the fish only skips the rod-size penalty.
  3. The hook, lure or fly body adds one step by its size against the fish (up to 15 cm, 16–35 cm, over 35 cm). A hook, bait or lure named for the fish makes it one step easier (they do not add up).
  4. Floats and sinkers never change the difficulty. They only decide which fish bite (the sinker route takes only 30 of the 72 fish).
  5. Rod +2 is how far you can cast (casting and lure rods cast shorter when your HP (stamina) is under 100). Rod +3 × 336 is the range line: how long you can leave a running fish before the line snaps and you lose the tackle. Neither makes a fish easier to land.

Big fish: timing is the meta, not the method Confirmed by test

  • With good timing, bait, casting and lure all land big fish (over 35 cm) about 99 % of the time, and equally fast.
  • With sloppy input (holding A, steady tapping, reacting late) every method lands 0 %.
  • Bait is the easy choice because it has no take step. Cast far or use a lure only because you like to.

The five methods

Float + bait Confirmed from the ROM

Good at Catches all 72 fish. Cheapest and simplest start; any bait the fish takes will do.

Weak at Float rods cast the shortest.

Must know

  • The float does not change the fight; buy the cheapest.
  • Choose the hook by fish size: small hooks for up to 15 cm, medium for 16–35 cm, large for over 35 cm.
  • A hook or bait named for the fish is one step easier.

Sinker + bait Confirmed from the ROM

Good at Needs a casting rod, so you cast far. Same fight as a float.

Weak at Only 30 of the 72 fish bite on the sinker route.

Must know

  • Check the fish takes sinker bait before you buy.
  • The sinker does not change the fight; buy the cheapest.
  • Casting rods take sinkers only; they cannot use floats.

Casting rod Confirmed from the ROM

Good at The longest cast. Lands big fish as often as bait with good timing.

Weak at Sold late, and the heavy casting rod costs ¥1,500.

Must know

  • It uses the sinker kit: same bite list (30 fish).
  • Rod +2 is the cast distance; below 100 HP (stamina) you cast shorter.
  • A heavy rod never makes the start easier.

Lure Confirmed by test

Good at Catches 38 fish and needs no bait.

Weak at Surface lures never took a big fish.

Must know

  • For fish over 35 cm use a SINKING type: spinner, spoon, sinking vibration, jig, soft claw, spinnerbait.
  • Topwater, shallow runner, deep diver and soft frog never took a big fish (0 of 240).
  • Press A FRESH inside the bite window. Holding A never takes a fish.

Fly Confirmed from the ROM

Good at Catches 33 fish. Small flies are cheap.

Weak at Worst start for fish over 35 cm.

Must know

  • Fly bodies have no big-fish size setting: 47 bodies make a big fish one step harder, 17 change nothing.
  • Wings and tails do not change the fight.
  • Fly rods are sold only in some stages.
