What this shop list proves

The listed items come from the original Japanese ROM's decoded six-area shop stock and traced menus. A ROM item price alone is not treated as proof of sale. Fly cards represent ready-made bundles at their full quoted bundle price; their body, wing and tail are not being presented as separate purchases. A listed position marks the decoded interaction or transition point; it does not promise a walking route unless the route is explicitly shown as verified.
