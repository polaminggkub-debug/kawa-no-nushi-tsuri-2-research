How to choose your gear

For every kind of item: what it does, whether it matters, how to pick one, and what not to waste money on. Short answers, no guesswork.

3 steps to pick a kit for any fish

  1. Pick the method

    A float rod with bait catches all 72 fish, so start there. Use a lure rod (38 fish) or a fly rod (33 fish) only if you want to.

    Skip the casting rod: it only catches bottom fish, and a float rod catches those too.

  2. Pick the rod and the hook

    Rod: enough reach for the fish. 38 fish are fine on any float rod, 15 need reach 24.

    Hook: the one named for your fish. No named hook: up to 15 cm a small hook, 16 to 35 cm a medium hook, over 35 cm a big hook.

  3. Pick what it bites

    Bait, lure or fly from the fish's own list, then cast so the bobber sits on the fish's exact tile.

    Not sure? The kit finder below picks all of it for you.

Why gear matters: the line-strain meter

Every fight has a six-step meter. Each mistake (pressing A at the wrong moment, see the fight simulator for the right rhythm) moves it up one step. At the top the fish is lost.

  • 1
  • 3
  • 7
  • 15
  • 31
  • 63

Your gear decides where the meter starts, which is how many mistakes you can afford. Start at 0, 1 or 3 and you land the fish almost every time. Start at 7 and it works for about 6 fish in 10. Start at 15 and almost everything gets away (median catch: about 1 in 70).

How we know

The numbers come from the original game's own code and from a port of its fight loop that matches real emulator runs frame by frame. Mistakes allowed and landing rates are simulated: 400 fights per case with the best A rhythm, a reaction time of 8 frames. They are not a promise for any single fight and were not tested on real hardware.

Which fish bite which bait, lure or fly comes from the game's tables and was checked in the emulator: a fish on the exact tile bit every time the item was on its list. The fly lock was measured from a blank save; a first boot on real hardware was not tested. Prices and shops were read from all six shops, the three special rod merchants and the fly makers in the real game.
