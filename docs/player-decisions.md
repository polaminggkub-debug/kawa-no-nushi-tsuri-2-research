# Player decisions from the ROM research

This page turns the confirmed item, shop-stock and gameplay findings into choices a player can make. The localized display copy is in [player-decisions.json](../data/player-decisions.json). Recommendations use only the supplied Japanese ROM's records, code, shop menus and observed game behavior. They are scoped to the effects actually established; compatibility is never presented as catch rate.

## A purchase to skip

Do not buy a fish-matched hook expecting more bites or easier landings. The hook changes the fight timing after a fish takes bait; depending on the setup, it can move the later escape/lost-equipment check earlier or later. It does not add bait compatibility. Choose the bait the fish accepts first. [Hook findings](hook-practical-research.md)

## Evidence boundary

This is a practical guide to verified choices, not a universal “best setup.” In particular, aim time measures how long the cast target can be adjusted; the rod's fish-position boundary affects one traced equipment-loss route; bait and fly lists represent compatibility checks; and none of those proves a bite rate or overall landing ranking. Raw ROM traces and addresses remain in the linked research notes for readers who want to audit the explanation.
